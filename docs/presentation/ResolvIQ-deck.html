02 / 12
The problem

Disputes are slow, opaque, and feel unfair to both sides.

Card Member

Files a dispute and waits weeks with no visibility. Provisional credit is uncertain; the reasoning is a black box.

Merchant

Learns of a chargeback after the fact. A $110–450 fee lands with little chance to respond early with evidence.

Issuer

Manual review doesn't scale. Regulation Z clocks (30-day ack, 90-day resolve) run whether or not anyone is looking.

This is a resolution problem — weighing already-disputed charges — not fraud detection.

03 / 12
The approach

Gather. Weigh. Resolve — transparently.

01 · Gather

Evidence assembly

Five connectors fan out in parallel — carrier, merchant, member history, transaction store, documents — each item hashed on arrival.

02 · Weigh

Fair-weighing engine

A deterministic scorecard, not an LLM. Exact additive contributions from both sides, gated by non-overridable hard rules.

03 · Resolve

Transparent decision

Plain-language narration checked by a faithfulness guard, chained into a tamper-evident ledger. Auto-resolve, split, or escalate.

The verdict is produced by an inspectable engine — a skeptical engineer can open devtools and watch it compute.

When the rules override the score.

Hard rules fire

→ The balance is pinned level. The lock, not the score, decides.

System refuses to auto-credit

Route: human_escalation

A friendly-fraud pattern is sent to a human — never silently paid. This is the fairness guarantee, made visible.

08 / 12
Transparent reasoning layer

Every claim traces to evidence.

Plain-language narration

Composed only from contributions and evidence — each sentence carries the evidence IDs it draws on. Two registers: plain for portals, technical for the audit view.

Faithfulness guard

Re-parses the narration and traces each factual claim to a live evidence ID.

✓ 12 / 12 claims traced
✗ unsupported claim → regenerate

If narration ever states something evidence can't support, the guard catches it — no hallucinated reasons reach a decision.

09 / 12
Tamper-evident audit

A ledger that refuses to be edited.

entryHash = SHA-256(prevHash + canonicalJSON(payload)). Alter any payload and the chain breaks at exactly that entry.

#1 3fa2…
#2 9c1b…
#3 ✗ 00d4…
#4 broken
#5 broken

Verified live on the audit screen: entries 1–2 green, entry 3 red, everything downstream flagged. Reset restores the chain.
